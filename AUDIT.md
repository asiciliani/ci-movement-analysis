# Audit and restructuring — October 2026

A code and methodology audit of the proof-of-concept found that none of the four claims presented
to the thesis director were supported by the code or the data. This file records what was wrong,
what was changed, and what remains open. The old scripts live in git history (commit before
`AUDIT.md` was added).

## Changes
* Two-stage pipeline (`run_tracking.py` → `run_features.py`); raw tracks saved once.
* BoT-SORT + ReID default; tracking diagnostics; camera ego-motion estimation and screening.
* Gap-aware Savitzky-Golay derivatives; no interpolation across gaps; no clipping.
* Body-length (or metric) units for every quantity; noise floor reported per video.
* SPARC/LDLJ on windows as the smoothness measures; Effort = jerk × KE removed.
* Within-video hypothesis tests with sign test, Wilcoxon, Cliff's delta, mixed models, surrogate
  tests for cross-correlation (`analyze_claims.py`).
* Proximity-graph analysis for jams (no neural network; honest name).
* Dataset inclusion rules and per-video exclusion reasons (`aggregate_dataset.py`).
* Curated acquisition with automatic static-camera / two-person screening.
* Removed: fabricated-phase figure scripts, HUD "claim" videos, mock VLM tracker, the zip, names.
* Tests: `tests/test_kinematics.py` (gap handling, noise floor, scale invariance, lag sign, surrogates).

## External ground truth (7 Oct, later)
| # | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 34 | **The method validated on public mocap with a known null.** Bigand, Bianco, Abalde & Novembre (2024, *Current Biology*; data CC BY 4.0, doi:10.48557/UR2GBG) recorded 35 dyads dancing freely, 60 s trials, 2x2 within dyad: visual contact (curtain or not) x same/different music, no touch. On 10 dyads (320 trials): (a) with **no channel at all** (curtain + different music) the naive per-trial circular-shift test is "significant" in 68 % of trials for speed and 18 % for vertical sternum velocity — the AUDIT 27 failure, now with real people; (b) the within-dyad contrast vision − curtain (different music) detects partner coupling in **vertical "bounce" velocity** (9/10 dyads, mean Δ peak|r| = 0.044, Wilcoxon p = 0.003), replicating the paper's finding, but **not in whole-body travel speed** (p = 0.12); (c) the bounce contrast survives degradation to a 2-D virtual camera at 25 fps with keypoint jitter of 0.005 bl (p = 0.010) and 0.02 bl (p = 0.019). Implications: the Phase-2 contrast is the right test; the statistic must include vertical (bounce) velocity, not only speed; the real effect (Δr ≈ 0.04, SD ≈ 0.06) is smaller than the design simulation assumed, and was detectable because each dyad contributed 8 one-minute trials per condition. | `analyze_external_bigand.py`, `outputs/external_bigand/report.md`, `data_external/bigand2024/fetch.sh` |
| 35 | Vertical "bounce" velocity added to `analyze_coupling.py`. On the CI clips, the one clip with many free-movement frames (swmrAJkYrlY, 1,820 frames) shows bounce coupling outside contact against the circular null (r = 0.25, p = 0.010), where speed did not (p = 0.21); vinlwkaIW4c is not significant (p = 0.11). This is **not evidence**: on the Bigand ground truth the same per-trial circular test calls 18 % of no-channel trials significant for bounce. It is the pattern to look for in Phase 2, nothing more. | `outputs/coupling/coupling_report.md` |
| 36 | **Lead/lag does not recover who leads, even with ground truth.** CoMPAS3D (Burkanova et al. 2025, CC BY-NC 4.0): improvised salsa, 9 pairs, 71 sequences, roles known. The lag asymmetry of the leader-follower cross-correlation (±0.5 s) puts the leader first in 7/9 pairs for pelvis speed (sign test p = 0.09; 46/71 sequences) and 3/9 for vertical velocity (wrong direction); after a 2-D projection with keypoint jitter it is at chance (5/9). Salsa leading travels through the hand hold with near-zero latency, so whole-body signal lags do not carry it. Consequence: H3c ("lead/lag identifies initiating/following") is dropped as specified; a leadership claim would need limb-level signals and a validated directional measure (e.g. transfer entropy on hand/arm kinematics), validated on this dataset first. | `analyze_external_compas.py`, `outputs/external_compas/report.md` |
| 37 | **Bigand et al. at full size (35 dyads, 1,120 trials).** Partner contrast (vision − curtain, different music): speed 27/35 dyads, Δ peak|r| = 0.034 (SD 0.045), p < 0.001; bounce 28/35, Δ = 0.030 (SD 0.043), p < 0.001 (with 10 dyads speed had looked null — power, not absence). Survives the 2-D/jitter degradation (0.02 bl: speed p < 0.001, bounce p = 0.0002). Naive per-trial test with no channel: 63 % (speed) / 22 % (bounce) of trials "significant". **Empirical power** (bootstrap over the 35 dyads, video-degraded, 8 one-minute trials per condition): 10 dyads 0.77 speed / 0.60 bounce, 15 dyads 0.93 / 0.81, 20 dyads 0.98 / 0.89; with 2 trials per condition 20 dyads give only 0.50-0.63. This replaces the assumption-based numbers of finding 32 for planning. | `outputs/external_bigand/report.md`, `outputs/external_bigand/power_empirical.md` |
